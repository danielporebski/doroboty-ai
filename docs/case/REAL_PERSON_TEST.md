# Real-person prototype test

Use a synthetic persona and example CV. Explain that the session tests the interface, not the person or their skills. Obtain consent and record only a synthetic participant code, date and prototype version.

If the session includes optional E01 audio, explain before recording that the tester may record only their own voice, that anyone with access to the same browser or an exported file can read it, and that a pre-interview containing personal audio and its transcript/report expires after seven days, with cleanup on the next app launch or while the app is open. The recording and transcript stay in browser storage and full-scenario exports. Reset replaces the scenario with baseline fixtures; clearing this site's browser data removes its local copy. Delete downloaded exports or recovery files separately after the exercise. Use an individual device or a separate browser profile. Do not record another person or include names, contact details or confidential information in audio or transcripts. Continue only if the tester agrees to use their own voice.

Ask the tester to find a relevant role, explain the employer need, prepare/edit an application, and submit it. Observe what they expect at each step without teaching the interface. In a separate task, switch to the employer and inspect the application.

Do not treat fixed Match Scores or simulated AI output as evidence of recruiting effectiveness. Record observed behavior, surprise, limitations, decision and next test in docs/learnings, without real personal details.
